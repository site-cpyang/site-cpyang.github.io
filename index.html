Biography

Chao Péter Yang is a machine learning researcher working on LLM alignment, agentic systems, and structure-aware generative modeling. He is a Research M.S. student in Computer Science at Yale University, advised by Prof. Rex Ying, and holds an M.S. in Data Science from Duke University, where he was advised by Prof. Cynthia Rudin, Dr. Stephen Ni-Hahn, and Prof. Brandon Fain. He is a first author at NeurIPS 2025 for ProGress, a structured symbolic music generator combining discrete diffusion with hierarchical music analysis, and his recent work audits and mitigates sycophancy and agreement bias in preference-aligned language models.

Previously, he was a Data Scientist Intern at Amazon Robotics, where he built an AI agent for warehouse root-cause investigation, and spent three years deploying production ML in financial analytics as a Senior Data Scientist at Curinos, Inc. He graduated with Highest Honors in Data Science from the University of Michigan and has served as a reviewer for NeurIPS and KDD.
